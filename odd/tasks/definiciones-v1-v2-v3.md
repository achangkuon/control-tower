# Apply the V1, V2 and V3 scope decisions

## Objective

Make the plan, the installer and the personal-instruction templates match the maintainer decisions of 2026-10-01 recorded in `docs/architecture/definiciones-v1-v2-v3.md`.

## Problem and why

The plan still calls the automatic Tower "V2", three open Sessions carry a hard 400-line maximum, two control pieces of later versions are still installed, and Codex and Hermes lack three personal-instruction blocks the other runtimes have. The maintainer wants a stable, organic V1 before planning Pista (V2).

## Scope

In scope (groups A and B of the decision record):

- A1, A2, A5: priorities and wording in `openspec/plan.yaml`; regenerated `openspec/ROADMAP.md`.
- A4: remove the hard 400-line maximum from the open Sessions `S174`, `S150`, `S173`.
- A6: verification only, already recorded in the decision document.
- B3: Codex and Hermes receive the documentation-close, plan-naming and plain-explanation blocks.
- B4: the installer stops installing `chanks-controls.ts` and `torre-firma.sh` and archives installed copies reversibly; `chanks-etapa.sh` and `pi-intercom` stay.

Held until the maintainer answers one open question (how V1 knows a Session is in progress without a formal start or dispatch marker): A3, B2 and C1, which touch `chanks-3-paralelo`, `chanks-3-session-kickoff`, `chanks-4-puente`, `chanks-5-session-doc` and Radar markers.

Out of scope: groups C and D, any live installation or agent restart, push to GitHub, Gentle AI managed assets, third-party Herdr files, archived Sessions under `openspec/archive/`.

## Constraints

- Nothing built for V2 or V3 is deleted: sources stay in the repository; installed copies are archived reversibly.
- Deferred skill sources under `agent-workflows/skills/` stay byte-identical where `tests/test_builder_flow.py` pins their sha256.
- No write to the real `HOME`. Installer checks run as dry-run or against fixture homes only.
- `openspec/ROADMAP.md` is generated by `radar render`; it is never edited by hand.
- Repository delivery policy: direct commit to `master`, no PR. Integration into `master`, installation and push are separate maintainer decisions.
- TDD strict. Runners: `bun test`; `PYTHONPATH=agent-workflows/tests python3 -m unittest <modules>`; `python3 -m unittest discover -s agent-workflows/tests`; `go test ./...` under `agent-workflows/tools/radar`.
