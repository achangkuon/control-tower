# S237 — `radar archivar <ID> --commit <sha>` cierra una Sesión en un solo comando

Subordinate execution continuity only, not a product plan.
Product Session: S237 (Torre V2 › v2-copiloto). Authority: maintainer-approved
copilot design (docs/architecture/copiloto.md, Tramo 6 and §7 paso 2); Engram 13295.

## Objective

`radar archivar <ID> --commit <oid completo>` validates the commit, registers
`entrega.commit`, archives the Session, runs `check` and `render` as one atomic
closure: either everything happens or nothing changes. Every rejection names the
step that is missing. Without `--commit` the command keeps working as today.
The compact historical format `{id, entrega: {commit}, historico}` does not change.
`chanks-5-session-doc` describes this single command.

## Authorized files

`radar/archivar.go`, `radar/archivar_test.go`, `radar/main.go`,
`skills/chanks-5-session-doc/SKILL.md`.

## Constraints

- Atomic closure across `openspec/plan.yaml`, the historical file and
  `openspec/ROADMAP.md`: a failed `check` or `render` restores the exact prior
  state and removes the file it created.
- The commit must be a full OID, resolving and integrated in the primary branch.
- The historical record and the compact reference must agree on the commit,
  because `archivedSession` re-derives the Session from the fenced block.
