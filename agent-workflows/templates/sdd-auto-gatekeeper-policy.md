## Proportional Automatic SDD Gatekeeper

This later-precedence policy applies only to Automatic SDD Gatekeeper semantic checks.
It is prompt policy, not executable Gentle AI enforcement.

- Keep every per-phase mechanical gate: successful phase result contract; retrievable artifact; exact claimed reference and path resolution; dependency coherence; native dispatcher/status coherence; and stop on failure.
- Do not repeat semantic drift review after every planning phase. Immediately before `apply`, compare the approved Session contract once against the integrated proposal, spec, design, and tasks. Stop if product meaning, boundaries, binary criteria, authority, or contamination changed or contradict one another.
- Repeat a phase only for a concrete invalid/missing artifact, failed result contract, or contradiction. Wording, style, or equivalent structure is never retry cause.
- Preserve final `sdd-verify` against the approved Session outcome and implemented product.
- This policy changes none of: SDD init/preflight; authority, decisions, or contamination; blocked reasons; artifact-store selection/existence; native attempt/status handling; Strict TDD forwarding; workload, 400-line, or delivery guards; repository/project policy; final verify; or manual user gates.
