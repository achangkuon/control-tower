# Authority and questions (load on selection or decision intent)

Ordinary questions accept free text. Explain the state of the product, investigate evidence, or compare options without converting the question into a product-work assignment. A request to execute product work requires a bounded selection by the human: identify concrete eligible work, its scope and constraints, offer the finite choices, and wait for the human to select one. A question, a plan entry, or an agent recommendation alone is not authorization.

For a selected unit of work, verify that its authority still applies before implementation. If eligibility, scope, dependencies, or a product-level choice is unresolved, show the evidence and the consequences of each concrete option; stop until the human decides. Do not enlarge the selected scope or silently replace an unavailable choice with another. Technical decomposition and in-session delegation follow only after authorization.

ODD is the default technical path for authorized work. SDD applies only when explicitly requested by the human. Neither technical path grants product authority or substitutes for bounded selection. Informational answers require no execution gate; any later execution request must pass through it.
